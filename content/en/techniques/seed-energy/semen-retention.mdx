## Why the traditions consider it important

**Taoism.** Jing is the 'oil in the lamp' of life. Each ejaculation spends some of that oil, especially with age. The physician Sun Simiao (7th c.) advised men to ejaculate no more often than:

- age 20 — once every 4 days
- age 30 — once every 8 days
- age 40 — once every 16 days
- age 50 — once every 20 days
- age 60 — best not at all, or once a month if very strong

Importantly, the Taoists **did not forbid sex** — they encouraged frequent lovemaking but **infrequent release**. The Yellow Emperor is said to have attained longevity by 'making love often but releasing little'.

**Yoga.** Brahmacharya is one of the five yamas. Yoga Sutras II.38: established in brahmacharya, *virya* (strength) is gained. Hatha Yoga Pradipika: *'Death from the falling of bindu, life from its retention.'*

**Ayurveda.** Shukra is the last and most precious tissue; its essence is Ojas, the basis of immunity and calm. Excessive depletion is considered a cause of Vata imbalance (anxiety, dryness, weakness).

**Tantra.** The seed is not 'bad' — it is sacred. So it should not be wasted but used consciously, uniting sexual energy with the heart and awareness.

## What is actually 'lost'

What many men describe after ejaculation:
- a sudden drop in energy and motivation, sleepiness;
- emptiness, sometimes sadness (post-coital dysphoria);
- reduced interest in the partner, withdrawal;
- with frequent ejaculation — brain fog, apathy, weak lower back and knees (in TCM, kidney Jing deficiency).

Physiologically this is linked to the prolactin surge, a dip in dopamine and parasympathetic activation. In other words, what is 'lost' is less a substance than **charge, potential and direction** — a force that could have been directed elsewhere.

## The key distinction: retaining ≠ suppressing

There are three paths:

1. **Release** — energy flows down and out.
2. **Suppression** — energy stays below but goes nowhere. Result: stagnation, tension, irritability, obsession with sexual thoughts, pain.
3. **Transformation** — energy is consciously **raised up the spine**, **circulated in the orbit** and **stored** at the navel. This is the Taoist and yogic path.

That is why retention is always practised **together with** raising practices (pelvic locks, testicle breathing, the Big Draw) and the circulation practice (the Microcosmic Orbit).

## For women

Traditionally women lose energy through **menstruation**, not orgasm. Women's seed (ovarian) energy practices:
- **Ovarian breathing** — energy is raised from the ovaries up the spine.
- **The doe exercise** and breast massage — regulate hormones, strengthen the pelvic floor.
- **Sahajoli mudra** (the female counterpart of Vajroli).
- Orgasmic energy is raised rather than allowed to scatter.

Note: women need not avoid orgasm; 'Slaying the Red Dragon' (reducing menstruation) is an advanced practice that should be taught by a teacher and is not for anyone planning pregnancy or with cycle disorders.

## How to begin — 4 stages

**Stage 1 (weeks 1–4): awareness.**
- Quit pornography.
- Note when and why urges arise (boredom, stress, loneliness?).
- Start 5–10 minutes of daily pelvic-floor work (see 'Pelvic Locks').

**Stage 2 (months 1–3): spacing and raising.**
- Reduce ejaculation frequency according to your age (above).
- Daily testicle/ovarian breathing and a short Microcosmic Orbit.
- When aroused — don't suppress; breathe and raise the energy up the spine.

**Stage 3: control.**
- Learn the Big Draw — stopping before the point of no return and lifting the energy.
- With a partner, practise slow, conscious lovemaking (Karezza, valley orgasm).

**Stage 4: cultivation.**
- Daily orbit, storing in the Dantian, meditation.
- Sexual energy becomes a steady inner source of warmth and light rather than a tension that must be 'released'.

## Common mistakes

- **Suppression without circulation** → pressure below, headaches, insomnia. Fix: orbit + bring energy down to the navel + movement.
- **Energy 'stuck' in the head** (heat, pressure, anxiety, insomnia) → focus on the feet and navel, walk, tongue to palate, cooling breath (Sitali).
- **Fighting yourself and shame** → relapse and guilt cycles. Treat the practice as training, not prohibition.
- **'Counting days' as the goal** → what you do with the energy matters more than how many days you 'held out'.
- **Inflated expectations** ('superpowers', 'magnetism') → disappointment. The real fruits are quiet: calm, clarity, vitality.
- **Perineal pressure during ejaculation** → retrograde ejaculation, not raising the seed.

## Signs the practice is going well

- More energy in the morning, better sleep.
- Warmth in the lower belly and lower back; a pleasant current along the spine.
- A calmer mind, less impulsiveness.
- Desire doesn't vanish but becomes manageable — felt as strength, not pressure.
- A warmer, more open relationship with your partner and with people.

## Simple anatomy

The pelvic floor is a muscular 'hammock' between the pubic bone in front and the tailbone behind. It has three regions:

- **Front** — around the urethra and genitals (PC, bulbocavernosus). You contract it when you stop the flow of urine.
- **Middle** — the **perineum**, the Taoist **Hui Yin** point ('meeting of Yin'), the yogic **Muladhara**. The orbit starts here.
- **Back** — the anal sphincter. You contract it in Ashwini mudra.

Being able to control these three parts separately is the first skill of seed mastery.

## 1. Finding and training the PC muscle

1. Once, while urinating, briefly stop the flow — that is the PC muscle. (Only to find it; don't do this routinely.)
2. Lying or sitting, contract it for 1 second, release for 1 second. **20 times.**
3. Then slow holds: contract 5 seconds, release 5 seconds. **10 times.**
4. Finally the 'elevator': contract in 3 stages (first floor, second, third), then lower slowly.
5. **Reverse Kegel** — gently 'drop' the pelvic floor downward as if starting to urinate. This teaches release — essential for a healthy pelvis.

Do this 2–3 times a day.

## 2. Ashwini mudra (horse gesture)

Named after the way a horse draws in its anus after defecating.

1. Sit upright, tongue to palate.
2. Rhythmically contract and release the **anal sphincter** — about once per second.
3. Do 30–100 repetitions. Feel warmth gathering at the tailbone and sacrum.
4. Advanced: inhale, hold the breath while contracting, then exhale slowly.

Ashwini 'pumps' energy into the sacrum, preparing it to rise up the spine.

## 3. Mula bandha (root lock)

Mula bandha is a gentle lift of the **perineum** (not the anus, not the genitals) inward and upward.

1. Sit (ideally siddhasana — heel at the perineum), spine upright.
2. Inhale. On the exhale, gently draw the centre of the perineum up, as if toward the navel.
3. Hold 5–10 seconds, breathing calmly.
4. Release. Repeat 10–20 times.
5. Over time Mula bandha becomes subtle — 10–20% effort, more attention than muscle.

The Hatha Yoga Pradipika says Mula bandha turns the downward flow of apana upward, where it unites with prana — awakening Kundalini.

## 4. Vajroli / Sahajoli mudra (gentle form)

Classical Vajroli is one of the most esoteric Hatha yoga methods, tied to preserving bindu. The safe, modern form:

**Men (Vajroli):**
1. Inhaling, contract the **front of the pelvic floor** (urethra, root of the penis), as if holding urine, and draw the genitals slightly upward.
2. Feel the testicles lift a little.
3. Hold 3–5 seconds, release on the exhale.
4. 10–30 repetitions.

**Women (Sahajoli):**
1. Inhaling, contract the urethral and vaginal openings and gently draw upward toward the uterus.
2. Hold 3–5 seconds, release.
3. 10–30 repetitions.

Vajroli/Sahajoli is the direct foundation of the Big Draw.

## 5. The triple lock: linking with the breath

Once all three parts are familiar:

1. Inhaling, contract in sequence: front → perineum → anus.
2. Feel energy drawn from the pelvic floor into the tailbone and sacrum.
3. Hold the breath 3–5 seconds, attention at the sacrum.
4. Exhaling, release completely.
5. 9 rounds. Afterwards, hands on the navel for 1–2 minutes to gather the warmth.

## Daily 10-minute routine

- 2 min — PC quick flicks (20) and slow holds (10)
- 2 min — Ashwini mudra (50 times)
- 3 min — Mula bandha with the breath (10 times)
- 2 min — Vajroli / Sahajoli (15 times)
- 1 min — reverse Kegel and full release, hands on the navel

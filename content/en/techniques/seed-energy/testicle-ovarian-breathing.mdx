## Preparation

1. Sit on the edge of a chair, feet wide on the floor, spine upright. Men — genitals hanging freely.
2. Touch the tongue to the palate behind the upper teeth.
3. Take a few calm belly breaths. Smile inwardly — relax the face, neck and shoulders.
4. Rub the palms together and place them over the genitals / lower belly — feel the warmth.

## Testicle breathing (men)

**Step 1 — breathing into the testicles.**
- Bring attention to the testicles. Inhaling, gently **lift the testicles** (cremaster muscle, as if drawing them up) and imagine breathing directly into them — they fill with cool energy.
- Exhaling, release; the testicles descend.
- 9–18 breaths. Notice coolness, tingling or heaviness.

**Step 2 — into the perineum.**
- Inhaling, lift the testicles and gently contract the perineum (Mula bandha). Imagine the energy from the testicles drawn into **Hui Yin** (the perineum).
- Exhale and release, keeping attention at the perineum. 9 times.

**Step 3 — into the tailbone and sacrum.**
- Inhaling, lift the testicles, contract the perineum and the back of the anus — **draw the energy into the tailbone**, then the **sacrum**.
- Tilt the sacrum slightly (pelvis rolls back) — this opens the 'sacral gate'. 9 times.

**Step 4 — up the spine.**
- Continue the same pumping, raising the energy in stages: **Ming Men** (on the spine opposite the navel) → **T11** (by the adrenals) → **between the shoulder blades** → **Jade Pillow** (base of the skull) → **Bai Hui** (crown).
- 3–9 breaths at each point.

**Step 5 — gathering at the crown.**
- At the crown, gently spiral the energy (with the eyes and attention) 9 times one way and 9 times the other. This 'nourishes the brain' (Huan Jing Bu Nao).

**Step 6 — bring it down and store it.**
- Tongue on palate. Let the energy flow down: between the brows → palate → throat → heart → solar plexus → **navel**.
- Hands on the navel (men: right hand on the belly, left on top). Spiral the energy 36 times clockwise (from centre outward), then 24 times counter-clockwise (from outside back to centre).

## Ovarian breathing (women)

**Step 1 — locate the ovaries.**
- Place the thumbs on the navel and form a downward triangle with the fingers. Where the little fingers rest is roughly the ovaries (the Ovarian Palace).

**Step 2 — breathing into the ovaries.**
- Focus on the ovaries. Inhaling, gently contract the **vaginal opening** (as if holding urine) and imagine drawing energy into the ovaries — they warm and fill.
- 9–18 breaths.

**Step 3 — into the uterus and perineum.**
- With each inhale the energy from both ovaries merges in the uterus (Ovarian Palace) and then descends to the **perineum**. 9 times.

**Steps 4–6** — as for men: tailbone → sacrum → up the spine to the crown → spirals → down to the navel.
- When spiralling at the navel, women place the left hand on the belly, right on top; 36 times counter-clockwise, then 24 clockwise.

## How this relates to 'raising the seed'

This breath is the first of three levels of raising the seed:

1. **Calm (cool) raising** — testicle / ovarian breathing, this article.
2. **Intensified raising** — testicle massage/compression, the deer exercise.
3. **Hot raising** — the Big Draw: when aroused, near orgasm, energy is raised with full force.

Start with the first: once you can raise and lower energy in a calm state, it becomes much easier when aroused.

## Map of the orbit

**Back channel — Du Mai (rising, Yang):**
1. **Hui Yin** — perineum (start of the orbit, gate of the seed)
2. **Chang Qiang** — tip of the tailbone
3. **Sacral hiatus** — sacrum (the 'sacral pump')
4. **Ming Men** — 'Gate of Life', on the spine opposite the navel (L2–L3); centre of the kidneys and Jing
5. **Ji Zhong** — T11, level with the adrenals
6. **Ling Tai / Shen Dao** — between the shoulder blades, behind the heart
7. **Da Zhui** — C7, base of the neck
8. **Yu Zhen** — 'Jade Pillow', base of the skull (the 'cranial pump')
9. **Bai Hui** — crown (hundred meetings)

**Front channel — Ren Mai (descending, Yin):**
10. **Yin Tang** — between the brows (third eye)
11. **Palate** — the tongue joins the channels
12. **Throat**
13. **Shan Zhong** — heart centre (middle of the sternum)
14. **Solar plexus**
15. **Navel / lower Dantian** — 3 finger-widths below the navel and inward
16. **Sexual centre** (Ovarian / Sperm Palace)
17. Back to **Hui Yin**

## The full practice: 6 phases

### Phase 1 — Rooting and the smile (3 min)
1. Sit on the edge of a chair, feet on the floor, spine upright, chin slightly tucked.
2. Tongue to palate.
3. **Inner Smile:** smile with the eyes inward — into the heart, kidneys, sexual organs. Taoists say the smile 'opens' the organs and makes sexual energy gentle rather than aggressive.
4. Feel the feet and the ground — energy needs a 'root'.

### Phase 2 — Lighting the fire in the Dantian (5 min)
1. Breathe into the belly: inhale, belly expands; exhale, it draws in.
2. Attention — 3 finger-widths below the navel, deep inside.
3. Imagine a small fire or a warm sun. With each exhale it grows a little warmer.
4. When you feel warmth, the 'fire' is ready. It is needed to 'steam' the seed — turn it into energy (the alchemists' image of water over fire).

### Phase 3 — Awakening the seed (5 min)
1. **Men:** testicle breathing — inhaling, lift the testicles slightly, breathe cool energy into them. 9–18 times.
2. **Women:** ovarian breathing — inhaling, gently contract the vaginal opening, breathe into the ovaries. 9–18 times.
3. Then direct the energy from the sex organs into the **Dantian fire** — 'the seed meets the fire'. This begins the Jing → Qi transformation.

### Phase 4 — Raising up the spine (10 min)
1. From the Dantian, bring attention down to the sexual centre and **Hui Yin**.
2. With each inhale, gently contract the perineum and anus (Mula bandha + Ashwini) and **lift the energy** to the next point.
3. Stay 3–9 breaths at each point until you feel warmth, tingling, pulsing or simply clear attention:
   - Tailbone → sacrum → **Ming Men** (stay longer — the home of Jing) → T11 → between the shoulder blades → C7 → **Jade Pillow** → **Bai Hui**.
4. At the base of the skull, raise the eyes upward and tuck the chin slightly — this helps the energy pass into the head.
5. At the crown, spiral the energy 9 times clockwise and 9 times counter-clockwise. **Huan Jing Bu Nao** — essence nourishes the brain.

### Phase 5 — Descending the front and circulating (10 min)
1. From the crown, let the energy descend to **between the brows**, then via the palate and tongue to the **throat**, **heart**, **solar plexus**, **navel**.
2. From the navel to the sexual centre and **Hui Yin**. The circuit is closed.
3. Now run the **whole orbit**: inhale — up the spine; exhale — down the front. 9, 18 or 36 rounds.
4. At first one round may take several breaths; later one breath = one round; eventually the energy moves by itself, without leading it with the breath.

### Phase 6 — Storing (3–5 min) — ESSENTIAL
1. Stop the orbit at the navel.
2. Place the hands on the navel (men right hand underneath, women left underneath).
3. Spiral the energy on the belly:
   - **Men:** 36 times clockwise (from centre outward, no wider than the ribs and pubic bone), then 24 times counter-clockwise (from outside back to centre).
   - **Women:** 36 times counter-clockwise, then 24 times clockwise.
4. Feel a 'ball' of warmth at the navel. This is your stored, refined seed energy.
5. Rub the palms, 'wash' the face, massage the kidneys and ears.

## How to tell energy is 'stuck'

- **In the head** (heat, pressure, throbbing, insomnia, anxiety): the energy didn't descend. → Lead attention down the front again, stay longer at the navel and the feet (Yong Quan point — centre of the soles). Take a walk, make a few long 'Hooo' exhales.
- **In the heart** (chest pressure, restlessness): → gentle cooling breath, smile into the heart, the 'Haaa' sound.
- **In the pelvis** (pressure, heavy testicles, arousal): the energy didn't rise. → More testicle / ovarian breathing and pelvic locks, testicle massage, movement.
- **Between the shoulder blades / neck** (tension, pain): the channel is still blocked. → Exercise, mobilise the spine (Spinal Flex), don't force.

## When you get aroused in daily life

Every spontaneous arousal is a **gift**, not a problem:
1. Don't judge or suppress.
2. Tongue to palate, a few slow breaths.
3. With each inhale gently contract the pelvic floor and lift the energy to the sacrum, then up the spine.
4. Bring it down to the heart — smile — then to the navel.
5. Within 1–2 minutes arousal turns into warmth, alertness and good mood.

## 100-day programme

**Days 1–30 — Foundation.**
- Morning: 10 min pelvic locks + 10 min testicle / ovarian breathing + storing.
- Evening: 10 min Inner Smile + orbit only up to Ming Men and back + storing.
- Fewer ejaculations, no pornography.

**Days 31–60 — Opening.**
- Morning: the full 6-phase practice (30 min).
- Evening: 9–18 orbit rounds + storing.
- Begin the cold Big Draw (9 times a day).

**Days 61–100 — Cultivation.**
- Morning: full practice (45 min), orbit up to 36 rounds.
- Evening: short orbit + still sitting in the Dantian (Wu Ji — emptiness).
- Warm / hot Big Draw as needed.
- Notice: warmth in the Dantian becomes constant — a sign that Jing is turning into Qi.

## Lifestyle that supports seed cultivation

- **Sleep:** early (before 11pm) — kidney Jing restores at night.
- **Food (Jing-nourishing in TCM):** black sesame, walnuts, bone broth, eggs, seafood (zinc), goji berries, black beans, pumpkin seeds. Less alcohol, sugar and cold food.
- **Movement:** Qigong, yoga, Tai Chi, strength training — energy must move.
- **Cold in moderation:** a short cold shower in the morning refreshes; too much cold, says TCM, weakens the kidneys.
- **Emotions:** fear (the kidney emotion) and chronic stress drain Jing; calm, smiling and nature protect it.
- **Creativity and service:** raised sexual energy naturally seeks expression — write, create, help others.

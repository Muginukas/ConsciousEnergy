## The Deer (men)

### Part 1 — Warming the testicles
1. Sit on the edge of the bed or a chair. Rub the palms until warm.
2. Cup the testicles gently in one palm (they rest in the hand).
3. Place the other palm on the lower belly, below the navel.
4. With the belly hand make 81 gentle circles (clockwise), feeling warmth spread into the testicles.
5. Switch hands and make 81 circles counter-clockwise.
6. Think of warmth gathering in the Dantian and the testicles.

### Part 2 — The deer's tail (contractions)
1. Inhaling, contract the **anal sphincter** and pelvic floor, as if drawing them up into the body.
2. Hold as long as comfortable (3–5 s at first), feeling energy rise into the sacrum and up the spine.
3. Exhaling, release.
4. Repeat 9–36 times.
5. Many feel tingling or warmth rising up the spine — this is the seed energy lifted by the 'deer's tail'.

### Part 3 — Storing
Hands on the navel, a few calm breaths, feel the warmth.

## The Doe (women)

### Part 1 — Warming
1. Sit so that the back of one heel presses gently against the perineum / vaginal opening (or place a rolled towel there).
2. Rub the palms until warm and place them over the breasts to feel the warmth.

### Part 2 — Breast massage
1. With the palms (or fingertips) massage the breasts in **outward circles** (hands rise up the inner side of the breasts and descend on the outer side) — 36 to 360 times. Traditionally this direction is said to 'disperse' and reduce menstruation and strengthen energy.
2. The **inward** direction (opposite) is traditionally said to stimulate breast growth — the outward direction is usually recommended.
3. Tongue to palate, breathe slowly, smile into the breasts and heart.

### Part 3 — Contractions
1. Inhaling, contract the **vaginal and anal muscles**, as if drawing them up into the body.
2. Hold as long as comfortable, feeling energy rise up the spine.
3. Exhaling, release.
4. Repeat until tired (9–36 times).

### Part 4 — Storing
Hands on the navel (left underneath), feel the warmth. Tradition teaches that it's worth directing some of the energy to the **heart** and **breasts** — a woman's energy centre.

## Brief history

- **Taoists** (Su Nü Jing, Mawangdui texts) described dual cultivation: the man 'receives' the woman's Yin energy, the woman the man's Yang; both raise and circulate it. Frequent lovemaking with infrequent ejaculation was advised.
- **Tantra** — maithuna as ritual: partners see Shiva and Shakti in each other.
- **1844** — John Humphrey Noyes (Oneida community) practised 'Male Continence'.
- **1896** — Dr Alice Bunker Stockham published 'Karezza: Ethics of Marriage' (Italian *carezza* — caress).
- **Today** — Marnia Robinson's 'Cupid's Poisoned Arrow' popularises Karezza as a way to avoid the dopamine 'hangover' in relationships.

## Preparation

1. A quiet, warm place, no hurry (at least 30–60 min).
2. Each partner separately for 5 min: pelvic locks and a few orbit rounds — the energy is already moving.
3. Sit facing each other. Gaze into each other's eyes for 2–3 minutes without speaking.
4. **Heart breathing:** each places a hand on the other's heart; breathe in sync for 3 minutes.

## The practice

### 1. Gentle touch (10–15 min)
- Caresses, massage, embraces with no goal of 'moving on to sex'.
- Lips, ears, neck, breasts, back, feet — awaken the whole body, not only the genitals.

### 2. Joining and stillness
- Join slowly. **At first, don't move** for 1–3 minutes. Just breathe and feel.
- A popular position is **Yab-Yum**: the man sits cross-legged, the woman sits on his lap with legs wrapped around him. Spines upright, hearts touching — energy rises easily.

### 3. Slow movement and '9 shallow, 1 deep'
- The Taoist rhythm: **9 shallow strokes, 1 deep**, then 8 and 2, 7 and 3 ... down to 1 and 9. This keeps the man from becoming too aroused too fast and stimulates the woman.
- When either partner nears a peak (8 of 10) — **both stop**, breathe, and the man performs the **Big Draw**.

### 4. Shared orbit
- When paused, each raises energy up their own spine to the crown.
- Then imagine a circuit between you: the **man** sends energy from his heart into the woman's heart, the **woman** from her sexual centre into the man's sexual centre (or the reverse — agree beforehand). The energy circulates through both bodies as one loop.
- Eyes, breath, smile — in sync.

### 5. The valley orgasm
- When arousal is held on a long 'plateau' (6–8 of 10) without peaking, **valley-orgasm** waves arrive: warmth, shivers, whole-body pleasure, sometimes tears or laughter, the sense of time dissolving.
- Not an 'explosion' but a **long, pulsing float**. Both feel the energy rising rather than draining away.

### 6. Closing
- Separate slowly, lie embracing.
- Each — hands on the navel, store the energy (36 + 24 circles).
- Share what you felt.

## How often — and is the seed ever released?

- In the Taoist view, men should ejaculate rarely — according to age and wellbeing (see 'Why Not Releasing the Seed Matters'). When it happens — consciously, with love, not out of habit.
- In Karezza, both partners avoid the orgasmic 'peak' — the aim isn't abstinence but lasting closeness.
- A couple can agree on their own rhythm: e.g. practise Karezza often and a 'full' union occasionally.

## When practising alone

The principles of Karezza and the valley orgasm also apply solo: slow self-touch, pauses at 7–8, the Big Draw, raising energy through the orbit, storing at the navel. Above all — **no pornography**: attention stays in the body and breath, not on an external image.

## The core idea

Male arousal rises like a wave toward the **point of no return**. Once that is crossed, ejaculation is inevitable. The Big Draw teaches you to:

1. **Recognise** the approach to that point (8–9 out of 10).
2. **Stop** and **lock** the gates (pelvic floor).
3. **Draw** the energy up the spine.
4. **Bring it down** to the navel and let arousal ease a little.
5. **Repeat** — each time the wave rises higher, and its 'peaks' become orgasmic without ejaculation.

## The 9 elements of the Big Draw (after Mantak Chia)

When you feel yourself approaching the point of no return, **all at once** (during one long inhale):

1. **Stop** moving (or stimulating).
2. **Contract the PC muscle** and the whole pelvic floor — 80–90% effort.
3. **Contract the anus** and feel it pull up toward the sacrum.
4. **Clench the fists** and **clench the teeth** (gently).
5. **Press the tongue** firmly to the palate.
6. **Tighten the neck and chin** — chin slightly back (this creates an upward pull).
7. **Roll the eyes upward** (as if looking at the crown) — the eyes lead the energy.
8. **Inhale** in short sips through the nose (9 sips), each time 'drawing' the energy: perineum → tailbone → sacrum → spine → back of the head → crown.
9. **Hold** 10–30 seconds, then **relax** the whole body and exhale. Feel the wave of energy up the spine and in the head.

Then:
- Spiral the energy at the crown (9 times).
- Tongue on the palate — let the energy flow down the front to the **navel**.
- Wait until arousal drops to ~5–6, and continue.

## Stages of learning

**Stage 1 — Cold draw (no arousal).**
Perform all 9 elements in a calm state, 9 times a day, for 2–3 weeks. This trains the muscles and nerve pathways.

**Stage 2 — Warm draw (during self-pleasuring).**
Stimulate yourself to 5–6, do a draw. Then to 7, draw. Repeat 9 times in a session. Stay well away from the point of no return.

**Stage 3 — Hot draw (near the point of no return).**
Once you reliably manage 7–8, go to 9. Stop, draw. Here many men first experience a **'small' orgasm without ejaculation** — a wave of pleasure and pelvic pulsing, but no release of seed.

**Stage 4 — With a partner.**
Same principle, slower rhythm, more attention to the breath. Agree on a 'stop' signal — your partner stops too.

## Non-ejaculatory orgasm: what it feels like

- At first — **'valley'-type waves**: pleasure without a peak, rhythmic pelvic pulsing.
- Later — **whole-body orgasms**: warmth, shivers along the spine, pleasure in the head and heart, sometimes tears or laughter.
- Afterwards the energy **doesn't crash** — you feel alive, not sleepy.
- The erection may remain, or subside and return — this is normal.

## For women

Women don't need to 'stop' orgasm. They use the Big Draw so that orgasmic energy **doesn't drop and scatter**:
1. Near or during orgasm — contract the vaginal and anal muscles, tongue to palate, eyes up.
2. Inhaling, draw the energy up the spine to the crown.
3. Let it descend to the heart and navel.

The result — longer, deeper orgasms that spread through the whole body.

## The finger technique — why caution

Some books teach pressing the perineal point (between anus and scrotum) with three fingers to stop ejaculation. Know that:

- Pressed **too late** (once ejaculation has started), semen flows into the bladder — **retrograde ejaculation**, not conservation.
- Pressed **before** the point of no return it can help — but muscular control is safer and more sustainable.
- Frequent long-term perineal pressure can irritate the tissues.

Recommendation: learn control through muscle and breath, and use the finger technique only as a rare back-up, and **before** the point of no return.

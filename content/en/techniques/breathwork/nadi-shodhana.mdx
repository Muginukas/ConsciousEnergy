## How to Practice

**Hand position (Vishnu Mudra):** With your right hand, fold your index and middle fingers toward your palm. Use your thumb to close the right nostril, and your ring finger to close the left.

1. **Sit comfortably** with your spine erect. Close your eyes.

2. **Close the right nostril** with your right thumb. Inhale slowly and fully through the left nostril for a count of 4.

3. **Close both nostrils** (thumb and ring finger). Hold briefly (optional: 1–4 counts for kumbhaka).

4. **Release the right nostril.** Exhale slowly through the right nostril for a count of 4–8.

5. **Inhale through the right nostril** for a count of 4.

6. **Close both nostrils** again. Hold briefly.

7. **Release the left nostril.** Exhale slowly through the left nostril.

This completes **one full round**. Practice 5–20 rounds, maintaining smooth, even breath throughout.

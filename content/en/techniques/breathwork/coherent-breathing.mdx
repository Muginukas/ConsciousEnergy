## How to Practice

1. **Set a timer** or use a breathing pacer app set to 5 breaths per minute (5.5 seconds inhale / 5.5 seconds exhale).

2. **Sit comfortably** or lie down. Place one hand on your heart if you like.

3. **Breathe through your nose** — inhale gently and fully for 5.5 seconds, then exhale gently and fully for 5.5 seconds. No pause, no hold.

4. **The quality of attention matters:** Rather than just counting, bring a feeling of warmth or appreciation to your heart area. This amplifies the coherence effect.

5. **Continue for 10–20 minutes.** Notice the increasing sense of stillness and presence.

You can practice without a timer by counting to six on each breath (roughly approximating 5 breaths/minute), but a pacer gives better results.

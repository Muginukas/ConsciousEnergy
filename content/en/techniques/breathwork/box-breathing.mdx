## How to Practice

1. **Sit upright** in a comfortable position, spine tall, shoulders relaxed.

2. **Exhale fully** through your mouth to empty your lungs completely.

3. **Inhale** slowly through your nose to a count of **4**.

4. **Hold** the breath at the top for a count of **4**.

5. **Exhale** slowly through your mouth or nose for a count of **4**.

6. **Hold** the breath at the bottom (lungs empty) for a count of **4**.

7. This completes one box. Repeat for 4–8 rounds, or continue for up to 15 minutes.

**Counting tip:** Count one-thousand-one, one-thousand-two... to approximate one second per count.

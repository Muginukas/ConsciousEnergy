## How to Practice

**Round 1–3 (repeat each round):**

1. **Find your position.** Lie on your back or sit comfortably. Never do this standing up or near water.

2. **Power breaths (30 breaths).** Inhale deeply through the mouth or nose, filling your belly first, then chest — a full, active inhale. Then let the exhale go passively, no forcing. Repeat this 30 times in a rhythmic, continuous flow. You may feel lightheaded or tingly — this is normal.

3. **Breath retention.** After the 30th exhale, let all the air out and stop breathing. Hold for as long as is comfortable — beginners typically hold 1–2 minutes, experienced practitioners 3–5 minutes or more. There is no force required; the retention is easy because your body is saturated with oxygen.

4. **Recovery breath.** When you feel the urge to breathe, take one deep inhale. Hold it for 15 seconds, pressing it gently into your chest. Then release.

5. **Rest.** Take a few normal breaths and observe the state of your body and mind before starting the next round.

**After your final round**, remain in stillness for several minutes. Notice the sensations, the clarity, the aliveness in your body.

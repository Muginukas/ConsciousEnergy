## How to Practice

**Learning the sound:**

1. Open your mouth and exhale making an "Haaa" sound, as if you're trying to fog up a mirror or a cold window. Feel the gentle constriction at the back of your throat.

2. Now try the same sensation with your mouth closed — a soft, oceanic hiss on the exhale.

3. Apply the same gentle throat constriction on the inhale — you'll hear a similar soft sound.

**In practice:**

1. Breathe only through the nose, maintaining the gentle throat constriction on both inhale and exhale.

2. Let the breath be slow, smooth, and continuous — like gentle ocean waves.

3. Use this breath throughout your yoga practice, or sit with it for 5–15 minutes of pranayama.

The sound is your anchor. When the mind wanders, the sound brings you back.

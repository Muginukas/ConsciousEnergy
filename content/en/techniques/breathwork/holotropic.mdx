## About This Practice

Holotropic Breathwork is a **guided group practice** that takes place in a protected setting with a trained facilitator. It is not something to self-administer from written instructions. What follows is an overview for educational purposes.

## What Happens in a Session

1. **Preparation:** Participants (called "breathers") lie on mats with eye shades. They are paired with a "sitter" who holds space and ensures their physical safety.

2. **The Breathwork:** Participants breathe faster and more fully than normal — not a specific rigid technique, but a sustained, connected, energetic breath pattern sustained for 1.5–2 hours.

3. **The Music:** Evocative music (specifically curated by Grof) creates a sonic container that supports the journey. The music shifts from activating to introspective to integrative over the session arc.

4. **The Experience:** The range of experiences is vast — emotional releases (tears, laughter, shaking), visions, somatic memories, states of profound stillness, spiritual openings, or re-experiencing biographical or birth memories.

5. **Bodywork:** Trained facilitators may offer light body pressure (with consent) to release physical holding.

6. **Mandala Drawing:** After the breathwork, participants draw a mandala to externalize their experience.

7. **Group Sharing:** A circle where participants can share (optionally) what arose.

To find a certified Holotropic Breathwork facilitator or workshop, visit the Grof Foundation or the Association for Holotropic Breathwork International (AHBI).

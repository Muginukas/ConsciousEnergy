## How to Practice

1. **Sit with a tall spine** — cross-legged, in vajrasana, or on a chair.

2. **Begin with a deep inhale** to prepare.

3. **The pumping action:** Exhale forcefully through the nose by sharply contracting your lower abdomen (pulling your navel toward the spine). The inhale happens automatically and passively as you release the contraction.

4. **Rhythm:** Find a pumping pace of roughly 1–2 breaths per second. Each exhale is active and audible; each inhale is silent and passive.

5. **One round:** 30 to 60 pumps, followed by a full exhale and then a deep natural inhale. Hold briefly if comfortable, then exhale slowly.

6. **Rest between rounds** — take 3–5 natural breaths before the next round.

Practice 3–5 rounds. With experience, increase to 100–120 breaths per round.

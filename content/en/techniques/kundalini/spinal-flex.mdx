## How to Practice

**Position:** Sit in Sukhasana (easy pose — cross-legged) on the floor. Place your hands on your shins or knees.

**The Movement:**

1. **Flex forward:** On the inhale, allow the chest to rise and open, the spine to arch gently forward from the base. The pelvis tilts forward (anterior tilt).

2. **Flex back:** On the exhale, allow the spine to round gently backward, the chest to fall slightly, the chin to tuck. The pelvis tilts back (posterior tilt).

3. This creates a continuous, rhythmic, wave-like motion — like a gentle camel or rocking motion.

**Breath and Mantra:**

- **Basic version:** Inhale as you flex forward, exhale as you flex back. Optionally chant *"Sat"* mentally on the inhale and *"Nam"* on the exhale.
- **Advanced version:** Maintain Breath of Fire throughout the movement.

**Pace:** Roughly 1 cycle per second, or slower for a more meditative practice.

**Duration:** 1–3 minutes as part of a warm-up. Can be extended to 11 minutes as a standalone practice.

**Ending:** Inhale deeply, hold briefly, exhale, and sit in stillness for 30 seconds before proceeding.

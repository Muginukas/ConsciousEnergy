## How to Practice

**Important distinction:** Breath of Fire is NOT Kapalabhati. In Breath of Fire, both the inhale and exhale are **equal**, **active**, and powered by the navel. The navel pumps IN on the exhale and OUT (relaxes) on the inhale — rhythmically, without pause.

**Step by step:**

1. **Sit in Sukhasana** (easy pose) or any comfortable seated position with a tall spine.

2. **Begin with slow exaggerated nasal breathing:** Feel the navel pull in as you exhale through the nose, feel it relax and expand as you inhale through the nose.

3. **Increase the pace** gradually until you arrive at a comfortable rhythm — roughly 2–3 breaths per second. The breath should be even on both sides.

4. **No pause** between inhale and exhale — it is continuous and rhythmic.

5. **Mouth is closed** throughout. The sound is a regular, rhythmic nasal sniff-puff.

6. **Continue** for the prescribed duration (typically 1–3 minutes as part of a kriya).

**To end:** Inhale deeply, hold, apply root lock (Mulabandha), then exhale slowly. Take several normal breaths before continuing.

A normal side effect is slight dizziness or tingling — these pass. Nausea or significant dizziness means stop.

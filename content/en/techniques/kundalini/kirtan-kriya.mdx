## The Mantra

**Sa** — birth, beginning, the Infinite  
**Ta** — life, existence, transformation  
**Na** — death, completion, change  
**Ma** — rebirth, regeneration, renewal  

Together: *Sa Ta Na Ma* represents the entire cycle of creation — the wheel of life, death, and rebirth.

## The Mudras

Each syllable is paired with a finger mudra:

- **Sa** — Touch index finger to thumb (Gyan Mudra — wisdom)
- **Ta** — Touch middle finger to thumb (Shuni Mudra — patience)
- **Na** — Touch ring finger to thumb (Surya Mudra — vitality)
- **Ma** — Touch little finger to thumb (Budhi Mudra — communication)

Repeat continuously throughout the practice.

## The 12-Minute Protocol

1. **2 minutes** — Sing aloud in a comfortable voice: *Sa-Ta-Na-Ma* with mudras
2. **2 minutes** — Whisper: *Sa-Ta-Na-Ma* with mudras
3. **4 minutes** — Silently in the mind only: *Sa-Ta-Na-Ma* with mudras (most powerful)
4. **2 minutes** — Whisper: *Sa-Ta-Na-Ma* with mudras
5. **2 minutes** — Sing aloud: *Sa-Ta-Na-Ma* with mudras

**Ending:** Inhale deeply, stretch arms overhead, spread fingers wide, shake hands, exhale. Repeat twice. Then let arms float down and sit in stillness for 1–2 minutes.

The mudra pressure points are maintained continuously — even during the mental (silent) phase.

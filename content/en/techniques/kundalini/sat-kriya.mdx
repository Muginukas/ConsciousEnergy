## How to Practice

**Position (Vajrasana):**
Kneel on both knees with the tops of the feet on the floor. Sit on the heels. If this is uncomfortable, place a folded blanket between thighs and calves.

**Arms:**
Raise both arms straight up alongside the ears. Interlace all fingers except the index fingers, which point straight up. Cross the thumbs (traditionally right thumb over left for women, left thumb over right for men — though this varies by lineage).

**The Practice:**

1. Take a deep breath in.

2. Chant **"Sat"** as you sharply pull in the navel point (apply Mulabandha — root lock — contracting the perineum, sex organs, and navel).

3. Chant **"Nam"** as you release the lock and let the belly gently expand.

4. Continue this rhythmic pulse: **Sat (pull in) — Nam (release)** at a rate of approximately 8 times per 10 seconds.

5. Keep the arms straight, the spine erect, and the gaze at the third eye point (between eyebrows).

**Ending:**
Inhale deeply, hold briefly, apply root lock. Exhale completely. Repeat twice more. Then relax in Shavasana for twice the duration of practice.

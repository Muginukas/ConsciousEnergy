## The Structure of Yoga Nidra

Yoga Nidra follows a systematic structure. It is best practiced lying down in Shavasana (corpse pose) with full physical stillness.

### 1. Settling In
Arrange yourself in Shavasana — lie on your back, arms slightly away from the body, palms up. Eyes closed. Allow the whole body to relax into the floor.

### 2. Sankalpa (Intention)
Plant a short, positive intention in the space of the waking mind before descending deeper. For example: *"I am at peace."* or *"I am open to healing."* The sankalpa is planted three times with full feeling.

### 3. Rotation of Consciousness
The guide systematically moves attention through different body parts in a specific order — right thumb, right index finger, middle finger, ring finger... through the entire body. This is not the same as the Vipassana body scan; it is faster, designed to bypass analytical thought and access the unconscious directly.

### 4. Pairs of Opposites
The guide invokes pairs of feelings: heaviness / lightness, heat / cold, pain / pleasure. You experience each briefly. This teaches the mind to remain equanimous across opposite experiences.

### 5. Visualization
Simple images or scenes are offered rapidly. You observe them internally without analysis.

### 6. Return to Sankalpa
The intention is planted once more, now in the fertile soil of the deeper mind.

### 7. Externalizing
Slowly return to full body awareness, to the room, to the world.

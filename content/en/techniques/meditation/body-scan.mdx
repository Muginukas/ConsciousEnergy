## How to Practice

1. **Begin in a comfortable position** — lying on your back is traditional. Eyes closed.

2. **Arrive** in your body. Take 3 deep breaths and feel the physical weight of your body supported by the floor or surface beneath you.

3. **Begin at the feet.** Direct your full attention to the soles of your feet. Notice whatever sensations are present — temperature, contact with the floor or socks, tingling, numbness, pulsing. Don't force sensation. Just observe.

4. **Move slowly upward.** After 30–60 seconds, move your attention to your ankles, then calves, knees, thighs. Spend time in each region.

5. **Through the torso.** Lower back, abdomen, chest, upper back. Notice the breath moving the torso. Notice areas of holding or tension.

6. **Hands, arms, shoulders.** Often tightly held without our awareness.

7. **Neck, face, skull.** The face holds enormous amounts of unconscious tension — jaw, temples, around the eyes.

8. **The whole body.** Finally, hold awareness of the entire body as a field of sensation. Rest here.

9. **Return gently** — wiggle fingers and toes, take a deep breath, and slowly open your eyes.

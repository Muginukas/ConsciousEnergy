## How to Practice

**Setup:**
- Place a candle at eye height, roughly an arm's length away
- Darken the room and ensure there are no drafts
- Sit in a stable, comfortable position with a tall spine

**The Practice:**

1. **Settle the body** with a few deep breaths. Let the eyes soften.

2. **Fix your gaze** on the center of the flame — ideally the bright blue point at the base of the flame, just above the wick.

3. **Hold the gaze steadily** without blinking. The instruction is to hold as long as possible before needing to blink — but don't strain.

4. When you naturally blink, simply return the gaze. Over weeks of practice, the time between blinks extends.

5. **Continue for 10–15 minutes** of external trataka.

**Inner Trataka:**

6. Close your eyes. Visualize the flame with the inner eye at the point between the eyebrows. Hold this internal image as long as it persists.

7. When the image fades, open the eyes briefly, gaze at the flame again to refresh the impression, then close and repeat.

Finish with several minutes of sitting in stillness with eyes closed.

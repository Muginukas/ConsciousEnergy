## How to Practice

### The Traditional Phrases
Repeat these phrases (or variations) slowly and sincerely, letting each phrase resonate in the heart space:

*"May you be happy. May you be healthy. May you be safe. May you live with ease."*

### The Sequence of Expansion

**1. Yourself** — Begin with yourself. Place your hand on your heart if helpful. Direct the phrases toward yourself:
> *"May I be happy. May I be healthy. May I be safe. May I live with ease."*

Sit with this for 2–3 minutes. This is often the hardest phase — we resist self-love. Stay with it.

**2. A beloved being** — Bring to mind someone you love easily and completely: a child, a parent, a dear friend, even a pet. See their face. Feel warmth toward them. Repeat the phrases:
> *"May you be happy. May you be healthy. May you be safe. May you live with ease."*

**3. A neutral person** — Someone you see regularly but feel neutral toward: a neighbor, a cashier. Extend the same goodwill:
> *"May you be happy. May you be healthy..."*

**4. A difficult person** — Someone with whom you have conflict or difficulty. This is advanced practice. Extend metta even here — not approval of their actions, but a wish for their basic welfare.

**5. All beings** — Expand the field to encompass all living beings in all directions:
> *"May all beings be happy. May all beings be free from suffering."*

Rest in the open field of goodwill for several minutes.

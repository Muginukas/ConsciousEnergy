## The Core Technique

### Step 1: Anapana (Preparing the Mind)
Begin by focusing attention exclusively on the area around the nostrils — the point where breath enters and exits. Observe the natural breath without controlling it. Feel the physical sensation of air touching skin. Develop sharp, focused awareness here for 10–20 minutes before proceeding to the body scan.

### Step 2: Body Scanning (Vipassana proper)
1. Begin at the crown of the head. Scan slowly downward through the body, moving your attention from one area to the next.
2. At each area, simply **observe whatever sensations are present** — heat, tingling, pressure, pain, pulsing, numbness, vibration — without reaction.
3. The key instruction: **observe with equanimity**. If a sensation is pleasant, don't cling. If unpleasant, don't push it away. Simply observe its nature.
4. Move from crown → face → neck → shoulders → arms → hands → chest → abdomen → back → legs → feet.
5. Then reverse from feet back up to crown. This is one complete pass.
6. Continue for the duration of your session.

### The Teaching in the Practice
Every sensation — no matter how intense — arises, persists for a moment, and passes. This is not a philosophy; it is a direct observation. Vipassana trains you to see this truth in your own direct experience, which gradually transforms your relationship to all experience.

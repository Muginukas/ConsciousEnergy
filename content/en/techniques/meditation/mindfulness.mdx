## How to Practice

1. **Find your seat.** Sit comfortably — on a cushion, chair, or floor. Spine tall but not rigid. Hands resting in your lap.

2. **Close your eyes** (or soften your gaze downward at a 45-degree angle if eyes-closed feels uncomfortable).

3. **Arrive.** Take 3 deep breaths to settle.

4. **Find your anchor.** Rest your attention at the sensations of breathing — the physical feeling of breath entering and leaving your nostrils, or the rise and fall of your belly. Not controlling the breath, just feeling it.

5. **Notice.** When thoughts arise (and they will — constantly), simply notice that you've been thinking. No judgment, no self-criticism. Just gently return your attention to the breath.

6. **Repeat.** The entire practice is this: wander, notice, return. Wander, notice, return. Each return is a successful meditation moment.

**Duration:** Start with 10 minutes. Work toward 20–30 minutes for deeper benefit.

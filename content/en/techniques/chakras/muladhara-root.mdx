## Grounding Practice

### Part 1: Earth Connection (outdoors if possible)

Stand barefoot on earth, grass, or sand. Feel the soles of your feet making contact with the ground. Take 10 slow, deep breaths. With each exhale, imagine roots growing down from your feet deep into the earth.

### Part 2: Muladhara Meditation

1. Sit comfortably, spine tall.
2. Bring awareness to the base of your spine and perineum.
3. Visualize a glowing red sphere of light at this point, warm and stable.
4. Chant the bija mantra **LAM** (pronounced "lum") 9 times, feeling the vibration at the base of the spine.
5. Affirm silently: *"I am safe. I am supported. I belong here."*
6. Sit in stillness for 5–10 minutes, resting awareness at the root.

### Part 3: Closing

Feel the weight of your body — the heaviness, the contact with the earth. Let yourself be held by gravity. You do not have to hold yourself up. Rest.

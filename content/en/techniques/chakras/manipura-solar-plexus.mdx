## Solar Plexus Activation Practice

### Part 1: Agni Stoke (Fire Kindling)
Practice 3 rounds of Kapalabhati — 30–60 rapid pumping breaths per round. Feel the heat building in the core. This directly awakens Manipura.

### Part 2: Manipura Meditation

1. Sit tall. Bring both hands to the solar plexus area.
2. Visualize a radiant golden-yellow sun in the center of your abdomen — blazing, warm, powerful.
3. Feel its heat, its light, its energy radiating outward.
4. Chant **RAM** (pronounced "rum") 9 times. Feel the vibration in the belly fire.
5. Affirm: *"I am powerful. I am capable. I act from my deepest truth."*

### Part 3: Will Practice
Choose one action you've been avoiding — one expression of your will you've suppressed. Make a concrete commitment to take it within 24 hours. Manipura awakens through use.

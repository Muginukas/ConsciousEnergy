## Crown Chakra Practice

### The Nature of This Practice

Working with Sahasrara is less about doing and more about releasing. All techniques are ultimately scaffolding — the crown chakra opens through surrender, through the dissolution of the practitioner into the practice itself.

### Part 1: Prepare the Foundation
Complete a full practice through all lower chakras, or at minimum, spend 15 minutes in grounded seated meditation, working through a lower chakra practice (especially Muladhara and Ajna).

### Part 2: Sahasrara Meditation

1. Sit in a comfortable, erect position. Eyes closed.
2. Let the entire energy system settle. The spine is a pillar of light.
3. Bring awareness to the crown of the skull. Imagine a thousand-petaled lotus blooming at this point — luminous, violet-white, opening upward.
4. Visualize a column of pure white or golden light descending from above, entering through the crown, illuminating the entire spine and energy body.
5. Chant **OM** once — long, slowly, until the breath expires. Then rest in the silence after.
6. In that silence, rest as pure awareness itself. Not aware OF something, but awareness itself.
7. If the mind grasps for something to meditate on, gently release. No object, no technique, no meditator. Just this.

### Part 3: Integration
After the meditation, sit quietly for several minutes before returning to activity. The descent from Sahasrara experiences requires time. Eat grounding foods, drink water, and be gentle with yourself.

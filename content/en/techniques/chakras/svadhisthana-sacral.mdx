## Sacral Chakra Practice

### Part 1: Fluid Movement
Begin with 5 minutes of intuitive movement — sway your hips, make figure eights with the pelvis, undulate the spine. Let the movement be fluid and pleasurable, not mechanical. Put on music that moves you.

### Part 2: Svadhisthana Meditation

1. Sit or lie comfortably.
2. Bring awareness to the area 2 inches below your navel.
3. Visualize a warm, luminous orange sphere — fluid, like water moving in sunlight.
4. Chant the bija mantra **VAM** (pronounced "vum") 9 times. Feel it vibrate in the lower belly.
5. Affirm: *"I am creative. I am fluid. I allow myself to feel and enjoy."*
6. Imagine the orange light flowing, adapting, moving — like water in a stream.

### Part 3: Reflection
Ask yourself: *Where am I rigid in my life? Where do I deny myself pleasure? What creative impulse have I been suppressing?* Let the answers arise without judgment.

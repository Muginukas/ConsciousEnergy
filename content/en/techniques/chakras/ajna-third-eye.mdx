## Third Eye Activation Practice

### Part 1: Trataka Preparation
Practice 10 minutes of candle gazing (Trataka) as described in that technique. This directly draws prana to the Ajna region.

### Part 2: Inner Trataka
Close the eyes. In the space between the eyebrows, hold the afterimage of the flame (or any bright point of light you have just gazed at). When the image fades, softly refocus the inner gaze at the Ajna point regardless.

### Part 3: Ajna Meditation

1. Eyes closed, inner gaze at the point between the eyebrows — gentle upward and inward.
2. Visualize a deep indigo or violet light at this point — clear, luminous, expanding.
3. Chant **OM** (Aum) 9 times. Feel the vibration resonating in the skull and third eye point.
4. Affirm: *"I see clearly. I trust my inner knowing. I am open to wisdom."*
5. Sit in receptive stillness for 10–15 minutes. Don't seek — just be open. Notice whatever arises in the space of awareness.

### Shambhavi Mudra (Advanced)
With eyes closed, roll the eyes gently upward toward the Ajna point. Hold gently — no strain. This is a classic Tantric eye mudra for third eye activation. Hold for 30–60 seconds at a time.

## Throat Opening Practice

### Part 1: Vocal Warm-Up
Spend 2–3 minutes humming — simply close the mouth and hum at a comfortable pitch. Feel the vibration in the throat and skull. Shift the pitch and find the resonance that feels most alive. This directly stimulates the vagus nerve and warms the Vishuddha center.

### Part 2: Vishuddha Meditation

1. Sit comfortably, spine tall. Rest your hands in your lap.
2. Draw attention to the throat center — the area between the collarbones and chin.
3. Visualize a luminous blue light here, clear as a summer sky.
4. Chant **HAM** (pronounced "hum") 9 times. Feel the vibration specifically in the throat.
5. Affirm: *"I speak my truth with clarity and kindness. My voice matters. I am heard."*

### Part 3: Expression
Write freely for 10 minutes about anything that has been left unsaid — to yourself, to someone else, to the universe. Don't edit. Don't judge. Just let the truth flow onto the page. Burn or keep the writing — the act of expression is what matters.

## Heart Opening Practice

### Part 1: Physical Opening
Begin with chest-opening yoga postures: Fish pose (Matsyasana), Camel (Ustrasana), or simply lie over a bolster or folded blanket placed beneath the shoulder blades. Breathe into the chest. Let it expand.

### Part 2: Anahata Meditation

1. Sit comfortably. Place one or both hands over your heart.
2. Feel your heartbeat beneath your hands.
3. Visualize an emerald green light filling the chest — warm, radiant, expanding with each inhale.
4. Chant **YAM** (pronounced "yum") 9 times. Feel the vibration at the heart center.
5. Begin Metta (Loving-Kindness): *"May I be happy. May I be held in love. May I forgive and be forgiven."*
6. Expand outward to others: to loved ones, neutral beings, difficult people, all beings.

### Part 3: Gratitude
Place your hand on your heart and bring to mind 3 things for which you are genuinely grateful. Not as a list — but as felt experiences. Let the gratitude land in the body. This is one of the most direct ways to open Anahata.

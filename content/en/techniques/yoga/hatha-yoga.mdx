## Core Principles

**Ahimsa (non-harm):** Never force the body into a posture. Pain is a signal to ease back, not push through.

**Sthira and Sukha:** The Yoga Sutras describe the ideal asana as combining steadiness (sthira) and ease (sukha). Neither slack nor straining.

**Breath first:** The breath is the teacher. If the breath is labored, you've gone beyond your edge.

## A Basic Hatha Sequence

1. **Shavasana (5 min)** — Begin lying still, arriving in the body.
2. **Sukhasana / Seated breathing (5 min)** — Simple seated pranayama to establish breath awareness.
3. **Cat-Cow** — Spinal awakening with breath.
4. **Adho Mukha Svanasana (Downward Dog)** — Full body awakening.
5. **Uttanasana (Standing Forward Fold)** — Hamstrings and lower back.
6. **Virabhadrasana I & II (Warrior I & II)** — Strength and grounding.
7. **Trikonasana (Triangle Pose)** — Lateral expansion.
8. **Setu Bandha Sarvangasana (Bridge Pose)** — Backbend and glutes.
9. **Supta Matsyendrasana (Supine Twist)** — Spinal detoxification.
10. **Shavasana (10–15 min)** — Complete integration.

For guided instruction, seek a certified Hatha yoga teacher. The physical details of each posture (alignment, modifications, contraindications) are taught in person and cannot be fully conveyed in text.

## The Vinyasa Sequence

The 'vinyasa' in vinyasa yoga refers to a specific linking movement:

**Plank → Chaturanga (Low Push-Up) → Upward Dog → Downward Dog**

This sequence is performed on the exhale-inhale between postures to reset the body between standing or seated poses.

## A Basic Vinyasa Flow

1. **Surya Namaskar A (3–5 rounds)** — Foundation warmup
2. **Warrior I → Warrior II → Reverse Warrior** — Strength and expansion
3. **Side Angle → Triangle** — Lateral body opening
4. **Balance pose** — Tree, Warrior III, or Eagle
5. **Vinyasa transition** back to Downward Dog
6. **Seated postures** — Forward fold, boat, seated twist
7. **Backbend** — Bridge or wheel (Urdhva Dhanurasana)
8. **Cool down** — Supine twists, happy baby
9. **Shavasana** — 10 minutes minimum

The breath instruction: **Inhale to expand, lengthen, or rise. Exhale to fold, compress, or lower.** Let this principle guide every transition.

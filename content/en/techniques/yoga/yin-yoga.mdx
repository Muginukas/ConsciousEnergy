## Core Yin Principles

**1. Find your edge** — Come into the posture to the point of feeling significant sensation, but not sharp or acute pain.

**2. Stillness** — Once at your edge, become as still as possible. No fidgeting, no microadjusting. This is how connective tissue yields.

**3. Time** — Stay for 3–7 minutes. Set a timer. The first 1–2 minutes are often the most intense; the tissue softens and the mind can quiet.

**4. Rebound** — After releasing, lie in Shavasana or a neutral position for 1–2 minutes before the next posture. The rebound is where the body integrates.

## Classic Yin Postures

- **Dragon (Low Lunge)** — Deep hip flexor opening; 3–5 min each side
- **Butterfly / Baddha Konasana** — Inner groin and lower back; 3–5 min
- **Sleeping Swan (Yin Pigeon)** — Deep external hip rotation; 3–7 min each side
- **Caterpillar (Seated Forward Fold)** — Entire posterior chain; 3–5 min
- **Sphinx / Seal** — Gentle backbend, stimulates kidney meridian; 3–5 min
- **Twisted Root (Supine Twist)** — Spinal rotation; 3–5 min each side
- **Legs Up the Wall (Viparita Karani)** — Nervous system restoration; 5–10 min

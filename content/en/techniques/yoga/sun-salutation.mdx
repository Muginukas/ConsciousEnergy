## The 12-Posture Sequence

Perform each posture on a breath:

1. **Pranamasana** — Stand tall, palms together at heart. Exhale.
2. **Hasta Uttanasana** — Inhale, sweep arms overhead, slight backbend.
3. **Uttanasana** — Exhale, fold forward, hands to floor.
4. **Ashwa Sanchalanasana** — Inhale, step right foot back into low lunge, look up.
5. **Dandasana / Plank** — Exhale, step left foot back, plank position.
6. **Ashtanga Namaskara** — Exhale, lower knees, chest, chin to floor (8-point posture).
7. **Bhujangasana (Cobra)** — Inhale, slide forward, chest opens, legs on floor.
8. **Adho Mukha Svanasana (Downward Dog)** — Exhale, tuck toes, lift hips, press back.
9. **Ashwa Sanchalanasana** — Inhale, step right foot forward between hands, lunge.
10. **Uttanasana** — Exhale, bring left foot forward, fold.
11. **Hasta Uttanasana** — Inhale, rise, sweep arms overhead.
12. **Pranamasana** — Exhale, palms to heart.

This completes **half a round**. Repeat on the other side (stepping the left foot back first) for the second half. Together: one complete Surya Namaskar.

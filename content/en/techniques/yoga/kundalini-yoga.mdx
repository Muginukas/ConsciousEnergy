## The Structure of a Kundalini Class

### 1. Tuning In (Adi Mantra)
Chant three times: *"Ong Namo Guru Dev Namo"* (I bow to the creative wisdom, I bow to the divine teacher within). This protects the practice space energetically.

### 2. Warm-Up
Spinal flexes, rotations, and neck rolls to prepare the spine for energy movement.

### 3. Kriya
A kriya is a complete set of exercises, breath, and mantra working toward a specific outcome (e.g., "Kriya for Elevation," "Navel Kriya"). Each exercise is held for a specific duration (often 1–3 minutes) with specific breath and arm positions (mudras).

### 4. Relaxation (Shavasana)
Deep relaxation after the kriya allows the energy to integrate. Often 5–11 minutes.

### 5. Meditation
A mantra-based or pranayama meditation, specific to the kriya's intention. Often includes mudra and eye position.

### 6. Closing Song (Long Time Sun)
Most classes close with this traditional blessing.

**Finding instruction:** Seek certified KRI (Kundalini Research Institute) instructors and the 3HO Foundation's resources for authentic practice.

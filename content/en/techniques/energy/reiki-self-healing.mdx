## The Five Reiki Principles

These were given by Mikao Usui as the ethical foundation of Reiki:

> Just for today, I will not be angry.  
> Just for today, I will not worry.  
> Just for today, I will be grateful.  
> Just for today, I will do my work honestly.  
> Just for today, I will be kind to every living thing.

Begin each self-healing session by reciting these slowly.

## Self-Healing Hand Positions

Remain in each position for 3–5 minutes. Trust your intuition — if one area feels like it needs more attention, stay longer.

1. **Top of head** — hands resting gently on the crown
2. **Eyes and face** — palms over closed eyes
3. **Temples and back of head** — hands cupping the sides of the head
4. **Throat** — hands lightly at the throat, not pressing
5. **Heart center** — both hands on the chest
6. **Solar plexus** — hands on the upper abdomen
7. **Navel / sacral** — hands on the lower abdomen
8. **Hips / root** — hands on the hip bones or thighs

For each position, breathe slowly and naturally. Imagine or intend that universal healing energy is flowing through your palms into that area. Notice sensations: warmth, tingling, pulsing, emotional shifts. Trust whatever arises.

## Closing
After completing all positions, sweep your hands lightly over your entire body from head to feet (several inches above the body) to smooth and seal the energy field. Then rub your palms briskly together and shake them out.

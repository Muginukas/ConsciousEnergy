## Understanding Prana

Prana exists in multiple forms:
- **Breath prana** — absorbed through the lungs with air
- **Food prana** — absorbed through fresh, sunlit, minimally processed foods
- **Earth prana** — received through bare feet on earth, through nature contact
- **Solar prana** — received through sunlight
- **Cosmic prana** — drawn through meditation and spiritual practice

## The Practice

### Part 1: Opening the Energy Body (5 min)
1. Stand or sit outdoors in fresh air, or near an open window.
2. Take 10 slow, full breaths, feeling your entire body expand with each inhale and soften with each exhale.
3. Shake the hands lightly for 30 seconds — this sensitizes them to energy.

### Part 2: Drawing Prana (10 min)
1. Sit comfortably, hands in your lap with palms facing up.
2. With each slow inhale, visualize luminous golden-white light descending through the crown of your head, filling the chest and entire body.
3. With each exhale, feel this light radiating out through every cell, through your skin, into your aura.
4. Sense the prana not as an idea but as a felt quality — warmth, tingling, fullness, electricity.

### Part 3: Storing Prana (5 min)
1. Bring both hands to the lower abdomen (Hara / Lower Dantian).
2. Imagine the prana you've collected gathering here — a warm, glowing ball of light.
3. Feel its warmth, its density, its aliveness.

### Part 4: Gratitude
Thank the sources of prana — the sun, the earth, the air, all of life. Energy work is relational; gratitude amplifies the circuit.

## The Two Channels

**Governing Vessel (Du Mai)** — runs from the perineum, up through the sacrum, spine, and over the crown of the head, ending at the upper palate.

**Conception Vessel (Ren Mai)** — runs from the tongue, down the front of the body through the throat, chest, abdomen, and perineum.

Together, these form a complete circuit. The tongue touching the palate is the bridge that closes the circuit.

## How to Practice

### Preparation
1. Sit comfortably, spine erect, tongue to palate.
2. Take 5 minutes of slow, deep nasal breathing to gather Qi in the Lower Dantian (navel center).
3. Feel a warmth or tingling at the navel.

### The Orbit

**Phase 1: Descend (Front)**
Guide awareness (and breath on exhale) downward from the navel, through the lower belly, to the perineum at the base of the body.

**Phase 2: Ascend (Back)**
On the inhale, bring awareness up through the perineum, into the coccyx, up through the sacrum, vertebra by vertebra up the spine. Through the neck, to the crown.

**The points to pause at:**
- Perineum (Hui Yin)
- Coccyx (Chang Qiang)
- Sacrum (Ming Men)
- Between shoulder blades (Ling Tai)
- Base of skull (Jade Pillow)
- Crown (Bai Hui)

**Phase 3: Complete the circuit**
From the crown, awareness flows over the forehead, down through the palate (via the tongue), throat, chest, solar plexus, navel.

### Speed and Pace
Begin with 9 complete circuits. Slow is better than fast. Each complete orbit may take one or several breath cycles — feel the Qi moving at a natural pace.

### Closing
After practice, gather energy at the navel by placing both hands there (left inside, right outside for women; right inside, left outside for men). Circle the navel 36 times clockwise, then 24 times counterclockwise. This stores the cultivated Qi.
